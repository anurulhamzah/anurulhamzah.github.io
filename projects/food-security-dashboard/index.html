Regional food security and vulnerability dashboard, Indonesia (2018-2024)

2025. Team of three. R Shiny, geographically weighted regression, ARIMA.

Period
2018-2024regency and city level, plus national GFSI indicators
Indicators
Food security index, stunting, undernourishment, poverty
Projections
To 2029against national RPJMN 2025-2029 targets

01Who it is for and what it answers

The dashboard is designed for policymakers, regional planners, public-health officials and analysts working on food security. It was built around three questions: which regions are priorities and what drives their vulnerability, how have trends changed and how do they compare globally, and which regions are on track to meet national targets (a food security index of at least 82 and undernourishment of at most 4.41%, from the national medium-term plan).

02Three tabs, three analytical tasks

  • General overview. 2018-2024 trajectories for the food security index, stunting, undernourishment and poverty, with interactive maps and multi-series trend lines.
  • Policy focus. Geographically weighted regression, shown as a diverging map, to find which socioeconomic variables are locally significant drivers of food security in each area rather than assuming one national relationship.
  • Future projections. ARIMA forecasts of the food security index and undernourishment through 2029, flagging provinces at risk of missing the national targets.

03Design and data decisions

The interface follows the Five Design Sheets framework, from raw data through to the final layout, so that each tab has a clear purpose and a consistent visual hierarchy for different audiences. The team fixed problems found during testing and cross-checked them in Shiny: zeros in the stunting and undernourishment data were recoded as missing values, with conditional rendering so that charts do not average over missing data, and a projection chart title and axis range that disagreed were corrected.

Code: Shiny app and user guide on GitHub. The datasets are not included, so the app does not run from the repository alone, and there is no hosted version yet, so no live demo link. This was a team project; the report does not attribute individual contributions.
