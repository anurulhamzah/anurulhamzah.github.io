Dashboards you can open and filter

Three business case studies with Tableau dashboards, each for a brief. Select a preview, or the button beside it, to open the live dashboard on Tableau Public, where every filter works.

The previews are screenshots, so this page loads nothing from Tableau until you follow a link. The dashboards are from 2023.

Airline passenger satisfaction

Tableau Public / 2023

  • Passenger and transaction totals, with gender, satisfaction and first-time versus repeat split.
  • Satisfaction by cabin class and by passenger cluster (The Executive, Senior Worker, Traveling Families, Junior Worker).
  • Pre-flight, in-flight and post-flight touchpoint ratings, filterable by class, cluster, satisfaction and gender.
Open live dashboard

A team case study (Team J, four people) in which I was project manager. The analysis used correlation, regression, cluster analysis and classification models.

E-commerce: executive and tactical dashboards

Tableau Public / 2023

  • Executive: revenue, average order value, orders, customers, sellers, monthly sales against orders, top 10 sellers, orders per quantity and customers by Brazilian state. Filters for product category, time period, city and state.
  • Tactical: revenue against accrued revenue, order trend, customer clusters on a map, top product categories by revenue with a cumulative share line, payment methods and delivery status.

HR attrition

Tableau Public / 2023

  • Employee count, attrition rate, and attrition against retention.
  • Breakdowns by gender, educational field, department, job role, age band and years at the company.
  • Survey heatmaps for work-life balance, environment and job satisfaction, job involvement and performance rating, with a department filter.
Open live dashboard

The brief: find which factors to focus on to bring attrition from 15% down to 10% within 12 months.
